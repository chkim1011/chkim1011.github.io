Hi, I'm Chanha Kim. I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Seoul National University, a member of the [Intelligent Motion Lab](https://imo.snu.ac.kr) advised by [Prof. Jungdam Won](https://sites.google.com/view/jungdam/home). Previously, I received my B.S. in Mathematical Sciences from Seoul National University in 2023.

I am interested in analyzing and synthesizing realistic 3D human motion — recently intrigued by how classical optimization can explore where data-driven approaches cannot reach. My broader interests include physics simulation, character animation, and computer vision.
